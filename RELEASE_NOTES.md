Download `RazerBatteryIndicator.exe` and double-click it. It installs itself: shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu, autostart, and an entry in Settings > Apps > Installed apps for uninstalling. No admin rights needed. If you already have 1.1.0 or newer, the program offers this update itself in the right-click menu.

Скачайте `RazerBatteryIndicator.exe` и запустите двойным щелчком, программа установится сама. Если у вас уже стоит 1.1.0 или новее, она сама предложит обновиться в меню по правому клику.

Windows SmartScreen will warn because the file isn't signed with a paid certificate: "More info", then "Run anyway". / SmartScreen предупредит, что файл без платной подписи: «Подробнее», затем «Выполнить в любом случае».

What's new in 1.5.0:
- English interface. It stays in Russian when Windows is in Russian or Belarusian. / Интерфейс на английском, если Windows не на русском или белорусском.
- Right click shows a check mark per device: untick one to hide it from the taskbar, the card and the warnings. / В меню по правому клику можно скрыть любое устройство.
- Bluetooth devices and DualShock 4 controllers are refreshed every minute instead of every 20 minutes; reading them sends nothing to the device. / Bluetooth-устройства и DualShock 4 обновляются раз в минуту.
- About a third of the CPU it used while idle: the widget is redrawn and raised above the taskbar only when something changed.
- An update that fails halfway no longer leaves the program without its exe, and settings survive a power cut while they are being saved.

1.4.3 brought back notifications from other apps (Telegram and others) while the widget is running.

1.4.2 picked up a connected, removed or swapped DualShock 4 within seconds and kept each controller's discharge history apart.

1.4.1 added DualShock 4 controllers over Bluetooth or USB. Over Bluetooth the level appears once Steam, a game or Windows has switched the controller to its full reports.

1.4.0 added Bluetooth devices of any brand, Logitech receivers and HeadsetControl headsets; those were tested without real devices, so please report anything that looks wrong.

Razer support tested on the Barracuda X 2.4 only.
