Download `BatteryTray.exe` and double-click it. It installs itself: shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu, autostart, and an entry in Settings > Apps > Installed apps for uninstalling. No admin rights needed. If you already have 1.1.0 or newer, the program offers this update itself in the right-click menu.

Скачайте `BatteryTray.exe` и запустите двойным щелчком, программа установится сама. Если у вас уже стоит 1.1.0 или новее, она сама предложит обновиться в меню по правому клику.

Windows SmartScreen will warn because the file isn't signed with a paid certificate: "More info", then "Run anyway". / SmartScreen предупредит, что файл без платной подписи: «Подробнее», затем «Выполнить в любом случае».

What's new in 2.1.3:
- New versions are looked for every 6 hours instead of once a day, so a PC that stays on sees a release the same day. / Обновления проверяются каждые 6 часов, а не раз в сутки.

2.1.2 showed Logitech mice on their charging cable, recognised any Logitech receiver and read the voltage-only G305, G703, G903 and G Pro Wireless.

2.1.1 added the Logitech PRO X 2 receiver and fixed a Logitech read that could stop all updates.

2.1.0 added a seven-day battery chart to the card and the "Tray icon" mode.

2.0.0 renamed Razer Battery Tray to Battery Tray, since it shows Bluetooth, Logitech, DualShock 4 and HeadsetControl devices too; an installed copy moves itself over when it updates.

1.5.0 added the English interface, hiding devices from the menu, Bluetooth and DualShock 4 refreshed every minute, and cut the idle CPU to a third.

1.4.3 brought back notifications from other apps (Telegram and others) while the widget is running.

1.4.0 to 1.4.2 added Bluetooth devices of any brand, Logitech receivers, HeadsetControl headsets and DualShock 4 controllers; except the Logitech PRO X 2, those were tested without real devices, so please report anything that looks wrong.

Razer support tested on the Barracuda X 2.4 only.
